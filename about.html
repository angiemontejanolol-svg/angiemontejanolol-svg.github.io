Angela Montejano is a second year Studio Arts with Preparation for teaching major at San Jose State University. Where she studies to acquire the skills to take her work to a professional level and build her portfolio. Angela would love to potentially work in galleries alongside mentoring students on how to cultivate their own art skills.

Montejano has a passion for painting and digital art that is shown in this portfolio. She primarily works in acrylic painting, digital art and sketching with graphite. However she has experience in multiple different mediums due to the countless art classes she has taken while being on her artistic journey. Montejano has experience with oil painting, watercolor, ceramics, inking,printmaking and digital softwares such as procreate and ibispaintX.

Originally from Stockton, California Montejano spent most of her days inside with nothing to do. She began drawing as a hobby with no intention to take it for anything more than that. However everything changed when she discovered a piece of media that changed her life. Montejano then began to be fascinated with the process of creating. She was inspired by how a piece of art could make her feel so many things. This then made her want to be an artist, to entertain and impact other people's lives. After this she began to take art seriously, she realized she was nowhere where she needed to be so she spent all her time perfecting her skills. After years of practice her skills began to improve, this motivated her to never stop creating.

Montejano believes the purpose of art and her personal goal is to evoke emotions out of the viewer. She believes the feelings do not have to be entirely positive as long as a work is inciting an emotion.She hopes to make art that inspires people to create, to remind them of the beauty and hardships of being alive.
